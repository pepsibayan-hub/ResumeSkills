# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 23, 2026_

This policy covers the **resume-skills-pepsibayan** plugin for Claude.

## What the plugin is

The plugin is a set of written instructions (skills) that help Claude with resumes, cover letters, job applications, interview preparation, and related career tasks. It has no server, database, account system, analytics, or tracking of its own.

## Data the plugin handles

When you use a skill, Claude reads the material you choose to share in your conversation, such as your resume, a job description, or details of a job offer. This can include personal data like your name, email address, phone number, and work history.

- **Collected by the plugin:** nothing. The plugin does not collect, store, or retain any data.
- **Sent to third parties by the plugin:** nothing. The plugin does not send your data to any outside service. Some skills may ask Claude to look up public information, such as a company's website, if you allow it.
- **Retention:** the plugin keeps no data. Your conversations are handled by Anthropic under [Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).

## Children

The plugin is not intended for people under 18.
